## Agent 22 — AI Explainability Agent: WOULD CATCH, and reveals a genuinely separable failure mode

određuju ishod (3-5, rangirani po uticaju)" alongside the percentage — meaning Strategy Engine's actual

## Agent 21 — AI Quality Auditor: WOULD CATCH the internal-consistency sub-domain; NO PRECEDENT for the other two

Read `routers/court_predictor.py:1164-1196` directly. Confirmed precisely: `nivo` (VISOKO/SREDNJE/NISKO) is
computed by `_calc_confidence_nivo()` (lines 1028-1082) from real counts (`rag_hits`, `vks_hits`,
`kancelarija_data`, `len(dokazi)`) — fully deterministic. Independently, `procenat` is generated by a
**separate GPT-4o-mini call** (lines 1169-1195, prompt at 1177-1184) with **zero cross-check against
`nivo`** — only clamped to `[0,100]`, never compared. A case could score `nivo="NISKO"` while the
independent GPT call returns `procenat=78`, with nothing in the code preventing or flagging that
contradiction. This exactly reproduces the internal-consistency failure Agent 21's charter names as its
own precedent. **Verdict on this sub-domain: WOULD CATCH, confirmed against live code.**

However, the charter's other two sub-domains have **no historical mission precedent** to validate against:
- **Cross-version stability** (does the same case, re-analyzed after a model/prompt change, produce a
  materially different conclusion) — no historical mission (Nexus, Sentinel, Atlas, Ledger, Migration,
  Phoenix, Keystone) ever ran a before/after model-version comparison on a stored case. `ai_forensics`
  (Mission Atlas) captures the data needed to do this check, but nothing has exercised it yet.
- **Cross-module contradiction** (Dashboard vs. Genome vs. Strategy Engine vs. Copilot disagreeing about
  one case) — Keystone's Phase 3 Golden Path trace found *connectivity* gaps (Genome not feeding Strategy
  Engine at all after initial pipeline run) but never checked for active *contradiction* between two
  outputs that both exist for the same case. Different failure mode, not covered by any historical finding.

**Verdict: PARTIALLY WOULD CATCH — strong, confirmed precedent for internal-consistency; genuinely no
precedent for cross-version stability or cross-module contradiction. Both remaining sub-domains should be
treated as untested capability, not assumed-working, until a future mission actually exercises them.**
